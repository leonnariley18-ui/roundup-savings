Shared components

Not part of the app — a bench for the two custom controls, which are pure interaction and cannot be covered by the unit tests. Both are custom because the native controls take the OS's appearance rather than the app's, and there is no native <select> or <input type="date"> anywhere in Ledger.

Date picker

Plain — any date
With a minimum of today
Check: Monday-start grid, serif month name, mono numerals, prev/next, a Today shortcut, accent-filled selection, and today marked distinctly from the selection. On the second field every day before today is disabled rather than refused after the click, and the Today shortcut is absent entirely when today falls out of range. Escape closes the popup and returns focus to the field without closing anything around it.

Surviving a repaint

The app repaints by assigning innerHTML. Handlers are registered by element id, so a field keeps working after the markup under it is thrown away and rebuilt. This button does exactly that to the first picker — it should still report changes afterwards.
